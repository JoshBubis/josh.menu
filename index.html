How it goes down

From “hi” to live, in five steps.

No mystery, no agency runaround. Here’s exactly what working together looks like.

  1. 01

    Say hi

    Use the form, the chat, or plain email. You’re talking to me — the person who designs and builds it — and you’ll usually hear back the same day.

  2. 02

    We scope it together

    A short conversation about what you need and what it’s for. You get one fixed price and a real timeline — no hourly meter, no surprise invoices.

  3. 03

    Sign online, kick off

    I send a plain-English contract you sign right in your browser, and a deposit locks in your slot. The whole thing takes about five minutes.

  4. 04

    I build, you watch

    Days, not months. You get a private preview link while it takes shape, and a round of revisions once the draft is ready — so launch day has zero surprises.

  5. 05

    Launch — and you own it

    Your site goes live on your domain, in a hosting account you control, with the source files handed over. You’re not renting the site from a page builder — optional care or hosting from us only shows up if we write it into the contract.

FAQ

Straight answers before you write.

What we build, how price and hosting work, and what the samples are for.

What do you actually build?

How your business runs — live chat, booking and scheduling, intake, payments, contracts, custom admin — plus the public face that matches. A focused lander with handoff is still on the Menu when that’s enough.

Is a focused landing page a one-time project?

Usually yes — a fixed price for design and build, then handoff into hosting you control. Ongoing care, Josh-hosted periods, or larger systems may be monthly — only when we write that into the agreement.

Who hosts the site after launch?

For most landers, the site goes into a hosting account you control — often free-tier Cloudflare Pages — with the source files handed over. Systems like chat or booking may stay on infrastructure we run for you when that’s part of the agreement. You’re not locked into a website-builder subscription just to keep a brochure up.

Are the sample sites real client work?

No — fictional demos that show different page shapes. The Menu leads with the kitchen, then the plates. Live product apps sit under Work.

Do you only build brochure sites?

No. The kitchen is the product — chat, scheduling, intake, payments, custom systems. Focused sites are one course. We’ve also shipped live product apps when the work calls for it.

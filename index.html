How it goes down

From “hi” to live, in five steps.

No mystery, no agency runaround. Here’s exactly what working together looks like.

  1. 01

    Say hi

    Use the form, the chat, or plain email. You’re talking to me — the person who designs and builds it — and you’ll usually hear back the same day.

  2. 02

    We scope it together

    A short conversation about what you need and what it’s for. You get one fixed price and a real timeline — no hourly meter, no surprise invoices.

  3. 03

    Sign online, kick off

    I send a plain-English contract you sign right in your browser, and a deposit locks in your slot. The whole thing takes about five minutes.

  4. 04

    I build, you watch

    Days, not months. You get a private preview link while it takes shape, and a round of revisions once the draft is ready — so launch day has zero surprises.

  5. 05

    Launch — and you own it

    Your site goes live on your domain, in a hosting account you control, with the source files handed over. I stay available for a short polish window and a functionality warranty if something we shipped breaks. Optional ongoing care or hosting only shows up if we write it into the contract.

FAQ

Clear answers before you write.

Price shape, hosting, what happens after launch, and what the samples are for.

What do you actually build?

Focused sites and the systems behind them when you need more than a page — live chat, booking, intake, payments, contracts, custom admin. A lander with handoff is still a great fit when that’s enough. Either way, you’re working with the person who builds it.

Is a focused landing page a one-time project?

Mostly yes — a fixed price for design and build, then handoff into hosting you control. After launch I generally include a short window for a few polish updates if something needs adjusting, plus a functionality warranty if something we shipped breaks. Ongoing care, Josh-hosted periods, or larger systems can be monthly — only when we write that into the agreement.

Who hosts the site after launch?

For most landers, the site goes into a hosting account you control — often free-tier Cloudflare Pages — with the source files handed over. You’re not locked into a website-builder subscription just to keep the site up. If chat, booking, or another system stays on infrastructure we run for you, that’s explicit in the agreement — and I’m still around if something we built stops working.

Are the sample sites real client work?

No — fictional demos on purpose, so you can feel different page shapes without confusing them for case studies. Browse all samples; live product apps sit under Work.

Do you only build brochure sites?

No. Focused sites are common, and the same studio builds chat, booking, intake, payments, and custom systems — plus live product apps when the work calls for it. Whatever we ship, the goal is something that holds up after launch, not a handoff-and-disappear.

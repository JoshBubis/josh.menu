Services

Four ways to work together.

Pick the shape that matches the problem — we'll confirm it on the intro call, not before.

  1. 01

    Fractional operating partner

    Step in as the ops lead for a season: run the weekly rhythm, unblock the team, and report to you in plain language.

    Best for teams of 10–40
  2. 02

    Strategy reset

    A focused sprint to re-answer who you serve, what you charge, and what to stop doing this quarter.

    Best for founders, 2–6 weeks
  3. 03

    Systems & SOPs

    Turn tribal knowledge into documented, repeatable process — the kind that survives a new hire or a vacation.

    Best for growing teams
  4. 04

    Advisory retainer

    Standing monthly time for the decisions that don't wait for a quarterly review. Usually follows one of the above.

    Best for ongoing input

Approach

How an engagement runs.

Three moves, always in this order — so you know what you're signing up for before you sign anything.

  1. 1

    Diagnose

    Two weeks. Interviews, a look at the numbers, and an honest read on what's actually the constraint — not just what's loudest.

  2. 2

    Design

    We agree on the plan together: three to five moves, in order, with an owner and a date attached to each one.

  3. 3

    Deploy

    I stay close while it ships — weekly sessions, fast feedback, and a clean handoff once it's running without me.

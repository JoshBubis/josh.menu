Approach

No maze. No mystery billables on day one.

You get a clear next step after we talk — retain, wait, or go elsewhere.

  1. 1. Tell us what’s going on

    A short note is enough. Deadlines matter — mention anything with a court date or filing window.

  2. 2. We scope the consult

    You’ll know if we can help, roughly what engagement looks like, and what to bring.

  3. 3. Decide with eyes open

    Retain if it’s a fit. If not, you leave with a clearer map — not a guilt trip.

Why Galveston

Texas’s homeport — short flights, easy drives, real Caribbean.

For a lot of Texas and Gulf Coast families, Galveston beats flying to Florida just to get on a ship. Three- to seven-night Western Caribbean sailings are the bread and butter: Cozumel, Costa Maya, Roatán, Belize, Progresso — plus weekend getaways when you only have a few days.

  • Drive-up embarkation Park near the pier (or we’ll help with hotels + transfers) and skip the airport scramble.
  • Houston-area airports Flying in? IAH and HOU are close enough that pre-cruise nights are easy to plan.
  • Lineup that actually rotates Carnival keeps several ships here year-round. Royal and Disney rotate favorites like Liberty, Mariner, Symphony, and Magic seasonally.
  • We still book elsewhere Alaska, Mediterranean, Bahamas from Florida — say the word. Galveston is the specialty, not the cage.

Who we book

Multi-line on purpose.

Cruise agents who only push one brand leave money (and better-fit ships) on the table. We compare across the lines that sail Galveston most — then go wider when your trip calls for it.

  • Royal Caribbean

    Liberty, Mariner, Symphony, and whatever Oasis-class heat is calling Galveston that season. Surf simulators, ice rink, Perfect Day add-ons when the itinerary allows.

    Galveston regular

  • Carnival

    Usually the deepest Galveston bench — Breeze, Jubilee, Dream, Horizon, and friends. Fun ships, flexible lengths, strong value for families and first-timers.

    Year-round presence

  • Disney Cruise Line

    Disney Magic and seasonal Galveston returns. Worth it when the kids (or the adults who never grew up) want character breakfasts and a calmer pace.

    Seasonal favorite

  • And the rest

    MSC, Norwegian, Princess, and luxury lines when they touch Texas — or when you’re departing Miami, Barcelona, Seattle, you name it. One advisor either way.

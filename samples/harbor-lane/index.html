How it works

Simple on purpose.

You won’t get bounced between strangers. One person learns the trip, then sticks with it.

  1. 01

    Send a few details

    Dates if you have them, who’s coming, and what “good trip” means for you — party ship, quiet balcony, kids club, whatever.

  2. 02

    We come back with options

    Usually two or three. We’ll say what we’d book for ourselves and why — including the boring stuff like cabin location.

  3. 03

    Book, then keep us in the loop

    We handle the booking paperwork. If something shifts before you sail, you already know who to text.
